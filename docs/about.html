Hi!! I’m Deya :)

I’m a Seattle-based cinematic wedding & family photographer.

My trajectory in photography has been a whirlwind; while I work in tech (like everyone else in Seattle!), my real passion lies in creating visual narratives that actively explores the richness and beauty of our everyday lives. I hope to add joy & meaning through art——something I think we’re fundamentally missing in today’s digital age!

I studied both CS & Film Studies at Carnegie Mellon, which is where my love for photo really began to grow. Having the opportunity to capture pivotal moments of celebration, love, and joy from people I would’ve otherwise never met has genuinely been such an amazing experience.

That said, it would be a pleasure to meet you and learn more about your story.
